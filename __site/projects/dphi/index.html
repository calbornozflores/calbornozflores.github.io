DPhi Changemaker

Project

Spanish translations of DPhi's deep learning curriculum — making practical ML education accessible to the Spanish-speaking world, starting with Convolutional Neural Networks.

Motivation

Most high-quality machine learning resources are in English. For students in Latin America — many of whom are highly capable but don't have strong English — this is a real barrier. When DPhi launched their open curriculum on deep learning, I saw an opportunity to bridge that gap for the UFRO community and beyond.

I've spent years mentoring students at Universidad de La Frontera and have seen first-hand how language barriers slow people down when the concepts themselves are already demanding.

Approach

Technical translation Jupyter Notebooks Deep Learning CNNs

The translation work went beyond word-for-word: I adapted examples and explanations to be culturally and contextually relevant for a LATAM audience, while keeping all the technical rigour of the original. The CNN module covers:

  • Convolution operations and intuition
  • Pooling layers and spatial reduction
  • Architecture patterns (LeNet, VGG-style)
  • Practical training with real datasets

Results

Impact: The translated materials are open on GitHub and have been used by students and self-learners across Spanish-speaking communities on the DPhi platform.

This was the first module in what I hope becomes a larger body of Spanish-language ML content. If you are a Spanish-speaking ML educator and want to collaborate on extending this, reach out via LinkedIn.
